# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Critique code, not people. No harassment, no
personal attacks, no dismissiveness about someone's experience level, no political or religious
campaigning in issues and pull requests.

## What is expected

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing opinions and accept that a maintainer may decline a change.
- Give and take technical criticism gracefully. "This breaks the backup step" is fine. "This is
  stupid" is not.
- Focus on what is best for the people using the tool.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, threats, or sustained disruption in issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, including home addresses, email addresses, tokens or
  screenshots of their configs, without permission.
- Dismissing a report of bad behaviour, or retaliating against the person who made it.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone is representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer, Adib Sadman, through
[GitHub](https://github.com/AdibSadman192) or a private security advisory if you would rather not post
publicly. Reports are read and handled privately.

Responses scale with the problem: a private word for a first offence, a request to edit or withdraw a
comment, a temporary block from the repository, or a permanent ban for serious or repeated behaviour.
The maintainer decides, and will explain the reasoning to the person involved.
